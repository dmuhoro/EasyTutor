# Learning Evidence and Mastery

## Evidence dimensions
recall, understanding, application, analysis, reasoning, evaluation, creation, transfer, retention.

## Weight principle
Different evidence types carry different weight. MCQ correct < explained solution < transfer in novel context. Weights are configured, not all-equal. Retention decays over time unless reinforced.

## Mastery confidence
Confidence = weighted, decayed aggregation of evidence across dimensions; requires at least application+transfer for "mastered" level; recall-only caps at "familiar".

## Prerequisites & difficulty
Prerequisite skills' mastery confidence must exceed threshold before unlocking dependent skill; adaptive difficulty uses demonstrated capability per dimension.

## Spaced review
Retention evidence drives ReviewSchedule; missed reviews lower confidence gradually (never silently).
