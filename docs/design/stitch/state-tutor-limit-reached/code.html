Wanjiru · Question #25
"Why doesn't the apparent surface area of contact change the maximum limiting friction force in automobile brake pads?"
EasyTutor Response

According to Coulomb's classical laws of friction, real contact occurs only at microscopic asperities. While a wider brake pad appears larger, the pressure per unit area decreases proportionally, keeping the net microscopic contact area constant:

F_max = μ_s · R_n Independent of Area
